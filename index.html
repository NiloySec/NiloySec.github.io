How I approach an investigation

CASE STUDY 01 · IMAGE RESEARCH

Image source & context verification

A repeatable workflow for checking where an image may have appeared and whether its surrounding context is consistent.

  1. Record the supplied image and research question.
  2. Search for visually similar and earlier public appearances.
  3. Cross-check dates, captions, landmarks, and source context.
  4. Report corroborated observations, uncertainty, and limitations.
Reverse image researchCross-checking
CASE STUDY 02 · DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

Public profile & username research

A scoped process for assessing public profile matches without assuming that similar usernames belong to the same person.

  1. Define identifiers and scope provided for research.
  2. Search relevant public platforms and indexed sources.
  3. Compare independent profile attributes and timestamps.
  4. Separate confirmed matches from possible leads.
Username analysisIdentity verification
CASE STUDY 03 · GEOLOCATION

Visual clue assessment

A cautious workflow for evaluating whether public visual and environmental clues support a possible location.

  1. List observable visual and contextual clues.
  2. Compare candidate locations against multiple references.
  3. Check for contradictions and alternative explanations.
  4. State confidence and what cannot be established.
Visual analysisConfidence notes
